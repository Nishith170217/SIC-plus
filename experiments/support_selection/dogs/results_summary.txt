Stanford Dogs SIC Support-Selection Experiment
===============================================

Checkpoint: results/dogs_bs1/sic_dogs.pth
Classes: 120
n_shot: 3
Retraining: No

K-means baseline:
- Top-1 accuracy: 78.4149%
- Top-5 accuracy: 96.8182%
- Macro F1: 77.5616%
- Balanced accuracy: 77.9529%

Compact representative supports:
- Top-1 accuracy: 78.7529% (+0.3380)
- Top-5 accuracy: 96.7599% (-0.0583)
- Macro F1: 78.0256% (+0.4640)
- Balanced accuracy: 78.3137% (+0.3608)

Maximum-diversity supports:
- Top-1 accuracy: 76.4685% (-1.9464)
- Macro F1: 75.0650% (-2.4966)

Per-class validation:
- Breeds improved: 37/120
- Breeds unchanged: 31/120
- Breeds declined: 52/120
- Mean class-accuracy change: +0.3608 points
- Paired t-test: t=0.5677, p=0.5713
- Wilcoxon test: W=1820.5, p=0.4565

Interpretation:
Compact representative support selection produced a small aggregate
improvement, but the per-breed change was not statistically significant.
Therefore, the Dogs experiment does not establish a general performance
improvement. Maximum-diversity selection reduced performance, consistent
with the Pascal VOC experiment.
