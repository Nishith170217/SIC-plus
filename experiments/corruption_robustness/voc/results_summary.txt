Pascal VOC 2007 SIC Corruption Robustness
==========================================

Methodology:
The trained SIC checkpoint and clean K-means supports were frozen.
No retraining or parameter updates were performed. Corruptions were
applied only to VOC test/query images before the B-cos evaluation
transform. Support images remained clean.

Clean baseline:
- sklearn mAP: 86.80%
- VOC07 mAP: 83.58%
- Label accuracy: 97.05%
- Micro F1: 76.96%
- Macro F1: 81.11%

Strongest tested corruptions:

Gaussian noise (sigma=0.20):
- sklearn mAP: 73.47% (-13.32)
- Micro F1: 61.56% (-15.40)
- Macro F1: 62.59% (-18.52)

Gaussian blur (radius=4):
- sklearn mAP: 62.06% (-24.74)
- Micro F1: 55.77% (-21.19)
- Macro F1: 48.62% (-32.50)

Central occlusion (40% area):
- sklearn mAP: 45.26% (-41.54)
- Micro F1: 31.95% (-45.00)
- Macro F1: 30.33% (-50.78)

Conclusion:
SIC is relatively robust to mild Gaussian noise and blur but degrades
substantially under severe corruption. Central occlusion causes the
largest reduction, indicating strong dependence on visible object
evidence. Label accuracy remains high because VOC contains many
negative labels; mAP and F1 provide a more informative robustness
assessment.
