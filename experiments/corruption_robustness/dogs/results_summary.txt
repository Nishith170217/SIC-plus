Stanford Dogs SIC Corruption Robustness
========================================

Methodology:
The trained SIC checkpoint and clean K-means supports were frozen.
No retraining or parameter updates were performed. Corruptions were
applied only to validation/query images before the B-cos evaluation
transform. Support images remained clean.

Clean baseline:
- Top-1 accuracy: 78.41%
- Top-5 accuracy: 96.82%
- Macro F1: 77.56%
- Balanced accuracy: 77.95%

Strongest tested corruptions:

Gaussian noise (sigma=0.20):
- Top-1 accuracy: 24.35% (-54.07)
- Top-5 accuracy: 52.40% (-44.42)
- Macro F1: 24.37% (-53.19)

Gaussian blur (radius=4):
- Top-1 accuracy: 30.42% (-48.00)
- Top-5 accuracy: 59.46% (-37.35)
- Macro F1: 29.79% (-47.77)

Central occlusion (40% area):
- Top-1 accuracy: 18.75% (-59.66)
- Top-5 accuracy: 43.58% (-53.24)
- Macro F1: 19.21% (-58.35)

Conclusion:
Stanford Dogs SIC performance degrades substantially under all tested
corruptions. Central occlusion is the most damaging, followed by severe
Gaussian noise and blur. The stronger sensitivity compared with the VOC
results is consistent with fine-grained breed classification relying on
subtle texture, facial, fur, and shape information. VOC and Dogs use
different primary metrics, so absolute metric drops should be compared
with caution.
