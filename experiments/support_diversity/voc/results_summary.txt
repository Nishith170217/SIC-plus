Pascal VOC 2007 SIC Support-Prototype Diversity Analysis
========================================================

Checkpoint:
results/voc_full/best_model.pth

Configuration:
- Classes: 20
- Prototypes per class: 3
- Total prototypes: 60
- Diversity: 1 - mean pairwise cosine similarity

Descriptive results:
- Mean diversity: 0.3062
- Most diverse class: sheep (0.8129)
- Most redundant class: boat (0.0181)

Diversity versus sklearn AP:
- Pearson r: -0.2755
- Pearson p-value: 0.2397
- Spearman rho: -0.3654
- Spearman p-value: 0.1131

Diversity versus VOC07 11-point AP:
- Pearson r: -0.2489
- Pearson p-value: 0.2899
- Spearman rho: -0.2872
- Spearman p-value: 0.2195

Interpretation:
Support-prototype diversity showed a weak-to-moderate negative
association with per-class AP. However, none of the correlations were
statistically significant at alpha=0.05. Therefore, this experiment
does not establish that prototype diversity improves or reduces
classification performance. Prototype representativeness, object
scale, background variation, multi-label co-occurrence, and class
difficulty may also influence performance.
